## Source ontology

### Top level

- - `event` something that occurred; events have a date, a type and a location; events can contain attributes and relations. 
- `entity` something that existed; entities have a type, they contain attributes and relations.
- `attribute` a quality or property of entities and events, at a given moment in time; attributes have a type and value and time domain (date or period). 
- `relation` a relation between entities and events; relations have a type and a value and a time domain (date or period). 

### Special types of `event` 

- `historical-act` is a record of an event. Specific records of events are specialization of the class: baptisms, marriages, burials. 
- `cevent` is an event as part of a chronology.
- `pevent` a personal event,  a type of event that can be included in a personal biography.

### Special types of `entity` 

- `occurrence`   one of possibly many manifestations of a past entity; most references to persons and objects in historical sources are occurrences of real persons and objects. So a given historical person or object can have appear multiple times in sources and in the database
- `person` an occurrence of person, with name, sex and id
- `object` an occurrence of an object, with name, type and id. 
- `geoentity` an occurrence of a geographic entity, with name, type, id.
- `real-entity` a real world entity; one to one correspondence with people, objects, geographic entities, institutions of the past; contains links to occurrences. *Real entities* are generated by aggreggation of *occurrences*, a process known as *Entity Resolution* or *Record Linking*. In a given dataset different users can have different visions of real entities.

### Special types of people

- `actors` people that play a role in an act or event. They may present at the act or have a funcional presence while physically absent.
- `kinship` people related to the *actors* by kingship relations. In some types of acts, like parish registers, people are ofter identified by enumeration of parents, and grand parents.

### Attributes
- `attributes` are records of characteristics of *entities* in a given point of time, or period of time. They are used to record information that changes over time, like *professions*, *places of residence*, *age*. 
- `relations`  are records of relations between *entities* at a given point in time, or period of time. Example of relations are: professional relations, friendship, kinship.
